Care

Looking after softwear

Light hands, cool water, space to rest. Soft tees and knits last longer when treated gently.

Cotton soft tees

  • Turn inside out. Machine wash cold, gentle cycle, mild detergent.
  • Avoid bleach and fabric softener—they dull colour and hand-feel.
  • Line dry flat or hang in shade; low iron on reverse if needed.

Light knits

  • Hand wash cool or use a knit bag on delicate. Do not wring.
  • Reshape while damp and dry flat away from direct heat.
  • Fold to store; hanging can pull fine gauges out of line.

Everyday notes

Spot-clean when you can. Rotate wear so fibres recover. If a snag appears on knit, do not pull—reach us for repair advice.
